MISSION CONTROL FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED ORBITAL QUESTIONS

What happens if I miss my launch window? +

Missed windows are rebooked automatically on the next synodic alignment. A $250M rescheduling fee applies, payable in stardust or one (1) first-born lunar rock.

Is the Vomit Comet really included? +

Yes. Every untrained passenger receives three (3) reduced-gravity parabola passes. Keep your breakfast down twice in a row and NASA awards you a commemorative sticker.

How much luggage can I bring? +

Up to 50 kg per passenger in a sealed vacuum canister. Any object that drifts away mid-transfer is legally classified as a donation to the orbital ecosystem.

Do you collect my personal data? +

Never. Stardance does not request email, phone number, age, home address, or SSN. Your manifest exists only in this browser — and on your titanium pass.

How do refunds work? +

Refunds are processed in 6–8 business months and delivered by carrier pigeon to your nearest licensed spaceport. Abort insurance expedites this to 4–6 months.
